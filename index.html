01 About

I’m Nimit Jain, a founder from Delhi building at the intersection of AI, content production and business systems.

Insturix started from a simple frustration: producing one strong piece of content still requires too many tools, too many handoffs and too much specialist knowledge. I’m working on replacing that fragmented workflow with one connected system, from the brief to the final published asset.

I care about leverage over busyness, systems over motivation, and execution that compounds. Outside the company, I write about building, lead teams, experiment with frontier AI and teach young students maths and science.
