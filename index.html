I’m Nimit Jain. I run Insturix: One tab, Entire Production. Funny enough, the first version was a creator insurance platform.

That’s a long story for another day. Today Insturix is vibe production, like vibe coding but for content: you say what you want and the whole production comes together.

I learned to sell before any of this. At my first company I sold 250+ tickets and led 120+ people across India, and I’ve since pitched investors at national startup events. Now I run product, sales, operations and hiring at Insturix, all at once. Off the clock it’s more sales and marketing, plus SalesOS, HUMAN 2.0 and theoretical physics.
